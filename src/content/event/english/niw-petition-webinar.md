Many NIW petitions are not denied because the applicant lacks talent. They are denied because the petition does not tell a clear, documented story. This live webinar from Capitol Law Partners walks through how an EB-2 National Interest Waiver petition is put together and where applicants commonly go wrong.

## What you will learn

- How the three-part Dhanasar test works and what USCIS looks for under each prong
- How to write an endeavor statement that is specific instead of vague
- What makes a recommendation letter useful, and who should write one
- Which kinds of evidence carry weight, such as publications, citations, funding, and independent coverage
- How priority dates and the Visa Bulletin affect timing after approval
- How NIW compares with other routes, including employer sponsorship and EB-1A

## Who should attend

This session is aimed at professionals who may qualify for EB-2 and are considering a self-petition. That includes researchers, engineers, physicians, scientists, entrepreneurs, and other advanced degree holders. It is also useful if you have already started gathering documents and want to know whether your plan holds together before you file.

You do not need to have an immigration case open to attend. Please note that the session covers general principles and is not a review of any individual case.

## Format

The webinar is presented live online by a Capitol Law Partners attorney. It includes a walkthrough of the petition structure, examples of common weaknesses in applications, and a live question and answer period. Bring your questions about evidence, timing, and strategy. Questions that depend on private facts are best handled in a one-on-one consultation.
