A ten-year green card expires, but your permanent resident status does not. The card is proof of status, not the status itself. Letting the card expire does not make you lose your green card status, but it causes practical problems with work, travel, and identification. Conditional green cards are different: when a two-year card expires, the status ends too unless you file to remove the conditions.

## How long is a green card good for?

- **Ten-year card**: issued to most permanent residents. Renew it with Form I-90 within six months of expiration.
- **Two-year conditional card**: issued for marriage-based green cards approved within two years of the marriage, and to EB-5 investors. You must file Form I-751 or Form I-829 to remove conditions in the 90 days before it expires.

## What happens if your ten-year card expires

You remain a permanent resident, but:

- **Employment**: Employers cannot accept an expired card for new hires on Form I-9, though they do not reverify existing employees.
- **Travel**: An expired card may not be accepted for reentry. Airlines may refuse boarding.
- **Licenses and benefits**: State agencies and banks may refuse an expired card.

There is no penalty fee for late renewal, but you should file the I-90 as soon as you notice. USCIS currently extends the validity of an expiring card with the I-90 receipt notice for a set period, which helps with work and travel.

## What happens if a conditional card expires

If you do not file the I-751 or I-829 on time, your conditional status can terminate automatically, and you may be placed in removal proceedings. A late filing is accepted only with good cause. File as soon as possible with a written explanation.

## Green card with no expiration date

Some older green cards, issued roughly between the late 1970s and 1989, have no expiration date. USCIS has said those cards technically remain valid, but it encourages holders to replace them. In practice:

- Employers and agencies may question them.
- They lack modern security features.
- Travel with them can lead to extra questioning.

Holders should file Form I-90 to get a current card. The older Form I-151 "alien registration receipt card," issued before 1979, is no longer valid proof of status and must be replaced.

## Green card expired ten years ago

If your card expired long ago, you are still a permanent resident unless you abandoned residence or were ordered removed. Before you file, consider:

- **Long trips abroad.** Extended absences can raise abandonment questions.
- **Criminal history.** Any arrests or convictions since the card was issued should be reviewed before filing, because the I-90 involves background checks.
- **Naturalization.** If you qualify, applying for citizenship may make more sense than renewing. You can file the N-400 with an expired card, though you will usually need to show you are renewing it or otherwise prove status.

## Card errors and name changes

If your name changed or the card contains an error, file Form I-90 to get a corrected card. USCIS errors can be corrected without a fee.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I travel with an expired green card?
It is risky. Airlines and CBP may not accept it. Carry your I-90 receipt notice if it extends the card, or get an I-551 stamp.

### When should I renew my green card?
Within six months before the expiration date.

### How long does it take to renew a green card?
Processing times vary and are posted by USCIS. The receipt notice usually extends your card while you wait.

### Is my green card still valid if I was abroad for years?
The card may still be physically valid, but long absences can lead CBP to question whether you abandoned residence.

### Will I lose status if I never renew?
No, but you will face practical problems proving status, and failing to carry valid evidence of registration is technically a legal violation.

### Does an old card without an expiration date affect naturalization?
You can apply, but many applicants replace the card first to avoid questions at the interview.

### Can USCIS take my green card if I renew with a criminal record?
Renewing does not create a new status decision, but the background check can reveal issues that lead to removal proceedings in some cases.

### What should I do if my conditional card already expired?
File the I-751 or I-829 right away with a written explanation of why it is late. USCIS accepts late filings only for good cause, so explain the reason clearly and include evidence.
