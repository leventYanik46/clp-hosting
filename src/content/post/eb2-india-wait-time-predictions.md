For applicants born in India, the EB-2 wait is measured in years, often many of them, because demand far exceeds the share of visas any single country can use. EB-2 India predictions shift from month to month because they depend on factors nobody controls in advance. The useful question is not "what will the date be in June," but "what can I do now that does not depend on the visa bulletin."

## Why the EB-2 India wait is so long

The law gives each employment-based category a fixed annual number of visas and caps any single country at about 7% of the total across family and employment categories combined. India produces a large share of employment-based demand, especially in EB-2 and EB-3, so Indian applicants queue behind each other. Your place in that line is your priority date, usually the date your PERM was filed or your NIW petition was filed.

## Why EB-2 India predictions keep changing

Projections for EB-2 India move because they rest on assumptions that change:

- **Unused visas from other categories.** Family-based numbers left unused one year can spill into employment-based categories the next. That spillover is unpredictable.
- **Demand that was invisible.** Applicants with old priority dates who were not yet in the data may surface when dates move.
- **Retrogression.** When too many people become eligible, the State Department moves the date backward to stop over-issuance.
- **Agency processing speed.** If USCIS approves fewer cases than projected, numbers can go unused, and the next year's math changes.

Take any EB-2 India prediction, including those from official sources, as an estimate built on this year's numbers, not a promise.

## What you can control

### Upgrade to EB-1

If your profile has grown, EB-1 is typically the most meaningful way out of the EB-2 India line. EB-1A covers extraordinary ability, EB-1B covers outstanding researchers with a qualifying employer, and EB-1C covers multinational managers and executives. EB-1 India also has a backlog, but it has historically been shorter than EB-2. You can file an EB-1 petition while your EB-2 case stays pending.

### Keep your priority date

Once an I-140 is approved, you generally keep its priority date for any later employment-based petition, even if you change employers, unless the approval is revoked for fraud, misrepresentation, or a material error. A new employer can file a new PERM and I-140, and your old date carries over. This matters a great deal in a decade-long line.

### Use AC21 to stay in H-1B status

If you are in H-1B status, an approved I-140 allows three-year extensions beyond the usual six-year limit while you wait. A pending PERM or I-140 filed early enough can allow one-year extensions.

### Consider a spouse's chargeability

If your spouse was born in a country with a shorter queue, you may be able to use your spouse's country of birth for chargeability. This is called cross-chargeability and it can change the math entirely.

## What does not help

- Filing a national interest waiver does not shorten the EB-2 India line. NIW cases are still EB-2 and wait in the same queue.
- Switching employers before the I-140 is approved can reset your priority date.
- Downgrading from EB-2 to EB-3 helps only when EB-3 India dates are ahead, which changes over time. It requires a new I-140 and careful timing.

## Planning around the wait

A long wait still allows planning. Keep your status documents in order, keep employment records that would support an EB-1 filing later, and track when your children approach 21, because the Child Status Protection Act calculation depends on specific dates.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will the EB-2 India date move this year?
It might, but no one can promise it. Annual movement has ranged from meaningful advances to no movement and even retrogression. Treat any single prediction as one scenario among several.

### If I change jobs, do I lose my place in line?
Not if your I-140 has been approved for 180 days or more and your new employer files a new PERM and I-140. You keep the old priority date. After an I-485 has been pending 180 days, AC21 portability can also let you move to a same or similar job without a new I-140.

### Does a pending EB-2 case protect my children?
Partially. The Child Status Protection Act can subtract the time the petition was pending from a child's age, but a long wait for the date to become current can still push some children past 21. Check each child's numbers early.

### Can I file an EB-1 and keep my EB-2 pending?
Yes. Running both is common. If the EB-1 is approved, you can use its date and category for the green card while keeping the EB-2 as a backup.

### Is consular processing faster than adjustment for Indian applicants?
The date in the visa bulletin controls both. Processing speed after the date is current varies by USCIS office and consulate.
