Alexandria and the rest of Northern Virginia are home to families from nearly every country, and their immigration needs are as varied as the community: marriage-based green cards, citizenship, asylum, removal defense, and status questions after years in the U.S. Capitol Law Partners serves individuals and families in Alexandria from offices in Washington, DC and Herndon, Virginia, both a short trip from the city.

## How we help families in Alexandria

**Family-based green cards.** Petitions for spouses, children, parents, and siblings, including marriage-based adjustment of status and consular processing.

**Citizenship.** Naturalization applications, interview preparation, language and disability exceptions, and Certificates of Citizenship for children.

**Asylum.** Affirmative applications with the asylum office and defensive asylum in immigration court.

**Removal defense.** Representation at master calendar and merits hearings, bond, and appeals.

**Humanitarian relief.** U visas, VAWA self-petitions, T visas, and Special Immigrant Juvenile Status.

**Delays and federal court.** Mandamus lawsuits for stalled USCIS cases and habeas petitions for detention.

## Local agencies and courts Alexandria families deal with

**USCIS Washington Field Office.** Located in Fairfax, it handles interviews for green card and naturalization applicants in much of Northern Virginia.

**Asylum office.** Affirmative asylum interviews for the region are handled by the Arlington Asylum Office.

**Immigration courts.** Northern Virginia removal cases are heard at the Annandale and Sterling immigration courts. Hearing notices state which court and whether the hearing is in person or by video.

**Application Support Center.** Biometrics appointments are scheduled at regional centers; the notice lists the location.

Knowing which office has your case helps set expectations for timing and procedures.

## When Alexandria families typically call us

- After receiving a Notice to Appear or a hearing notice.
- Before filing a marriage-based green card when one spouse has a past overstay or entered without inspection.
- When a USCIS case has been pending far longer than posted processing times.
- When a family member is detained.
- When a child is approaching 21 in a pending family case.
- Before traveling abroad on a pending application.

## What to expect at a consultation

Bring passports, any immigration documents, notices from USCIS or the court, and a list of every entry to and departure from the U.S. We review your history, explain your options and their risks, and give you a clear plan with costs.

Consultations are available in person and remotely, and our team speaks several languages, including Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, and French.

## Choosing an immigration lawyer near you

Look for a licensed attorney, a written fee agreement, and a clear explanation of risks. Avoid anyone who guarantees results or offers to file without reviewing your full history.
