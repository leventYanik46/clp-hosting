The cost of an initial L-1 petition comes from three places: USCIS filing fees, additional statutory fees that apply to some employers, and legal and preparation costs. Processing takes either a few weeks with premium processing or several months without it. Budgeting both money and time up front keeps a transfer from slipping.

## L-1 government fees

USCIS fees change by rule, so check the current fee schedule before filing. The components for an initial L-1 petition on Form I-129 are:

- **Base filing fee** for the L classification.
- **Fraud prevention and detection fee.** Required for initial L-1 petitions and for petitions to change employers.
- **Asylum Program Fee.** Introduced in 2024. The amount is reduced for small employers and waived for nonprofits.
- **Public Law 114-113 fee.** An additional fee of $4,500 for employers with 50 or more U.S. employees when more than half are in H-1B or L status. Most smaller companies are not subject to it.
- **Premium processing fee**, if you choose it.

If the employee will apply for a visa at a consulate, there is also the visa application fee and, for some nationalities, a reciprocity fee charged by the State Department.

## Legal and preparation costs

Legal fees depend on the complexity of the case:

- An L-1A for an established executive at a large company is usually more straightforward.
- An L-1B for specialized knowledge requires detailed proof of what is specialized about the employee's knowledge, which takes more work.
- A new-office L-1 requires a business plan, proof of U.S. premises, and evidence of funding and organizational structure.

Some companies also pay for business plan writers, translations, credential evaluations, and expert letters.

## L-1 visa processing time

**Regular processing.** Times vary by service center and change through the year. USCIS posts current processing times online.

**Premium processing.** USCIS takes action within 15 business days of receipt: an approval, a denial, or a request for evidence. If a request for evidence is issued, the clock restarts once USCIS receives the response.

After approval, an employee outside the U.S. needs a visa interview, which adds consular wait time. Canadian citizens can apply for L-1 status at a port of entry or preclearance facility in most cases, which can shorten the timeline.

## Blanket L petitions

Large multinational companies that qualify for a blanket L approval can bring employees in without filing an individual petition with USCIS each time. The employee applies directly at the consulate. Blanket L saves time and USCIS fees for frequent transfers, but it is only available to companies that meet size and structure thresholds.

## New-office L-1 timelines

A new-office L-1 is usually approved for one year at first. Within that year the U.S. company must grow to support an executive or manager, or for L-1B, a specialized-knowledge role. The extension filing must show real operations, staff, and revenue or meaningful activity. Plan that extension from the first day, because evidence gathered in month eleven is often thin.

## Ways to reduce L-1 cost and delay

- Confirm the employee's one year of qualifying employment abroad before you start.
- Collect organizational charts, payroll records, and corporate documents early.
- Use premium processing only when the start date or travel plans require it.
- For a new office, secure the lease and funding before filing, not after.

## What an L-1 does not cover

L-1 approval covers the specific employee. Spouses and children need L-2 status. L-2 spouses are authorized to work incident to status, and their I-94 should show that.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who pays the L-1 fees?
The employer files the petition and generally pays the filing fees. The fraud prevention fee and the Public Law 114-113 fee, when they apply, are employer obligations.

### Is premium processing worth it for an L-1?
It is worth it when a start date or move is fixed, or when you want early notice of a request for evidence. It does not make approval more likely.

### Can an L-1 be extended?
Yes. L-1A workers can stay up to seven years total and L-1B workers up to five, filed in extensions of up to two years after the initial period.

### Can a small company use the L-1?
Yes, if it has a qualifying relationship with the foreign company and the employee meets the requirements. Size affects some fees, not eligibility.

### How long does the consular interview add?
It depends on appointment wait times at the post. Check the consulate's current wait times before setting a start date.

### Can the employee start working on approval?
An employee outside the U.S. can start only after receiving the L-1 visa and entering. An employee changing status inside the U.S. can start on the approved start date.

### Does a new-office L-1 cost more?
The government fees are the same, but legal and preparation costs are usually higher because of the business plan, premises, and funding evidence.
