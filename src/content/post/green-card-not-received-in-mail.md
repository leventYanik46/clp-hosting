After USCIS approves a green card application, the physical card is produced and mailed separately. Most cards arrive within a few weeks of the "Card Was Mailed To Me" status update, but delays and lost cards happen. If your card has not arrived, there are specific steps to take, and some carry deadlines.

## When does the green card come in the mail?

The typical sequence after approval:

1. **Case approved.** You may receive an approval notice by mail.
2. **New card is being produced.** USCIS prints the card.
3. **Card was mailed to me.** Often with a USPS tracking number visible in your case status.
4. **Card was picked up by the U.S. Postal Service** or **card was delivered.**

The time between approval and mailing varies. Once mailed, cards are usually delivered within days to a couple of weeks. Track the USPS number if your case status shows one.

## If you entered on an immigrant visa

People who immigrate through consular processing pay the USCIS immigrant fee after receiving the visa. The card is mailed to the U.S. address listed with the visa. If you have not paid the fee, the card will not be produced. Your passport's immigrant visa stamp serves as temporary proof of permanent residence for one year from entry.

## Steps if your green card has not arrived

**Check your address.** The card goes to the address on file. If you moved, it may have gone to your old address. Update your address with USCIS and with USPS mail forwarding.

**Check USPS tracking.** If delivered, check with neighbors, building management, and your local post office.

**Wait a reasonable period, then contact USCIS.** If the card shows as mailed but has not arrived after about 30 days, contact the USCIS Contact Center. USCIS has a non-delivery inquiry process that is time-limited, so do not wait months.

**If the card was returned to USCIS.** Undeliverable cards may be returned. USCIS may hold them for a limited time before destroying them. Contact USCIS quickly to arrange re-mailing to the correct address.

## When you must file Form I-90

If the card was mailed to the right address but never received, and USCIS does not resolve it through a non-delivery inquiry, you may need to file Form I-90 to replace it. If the card was sent to the correct address on file and lost in the mail, you may have to pay the filing fee. If USCIS sent it to the wrong address because of its own error, or you updated your address in time, a fee may not be required.

## Proving status while you wait

You are a permanent resident from the approval date. To prove it while waiting:

- Keep your approval notice.
- If you need proof for work or travel urgently, request an appointment at a USCIS field office through the Contact Center for an I-551 stamp in your passport.

## Avoiding delivery problems

- File Form AR-11 or update your address online within ten days of moving.
- Make sure your name is on your mailbox.
- If you are waiting for a card, avoid moving if possible.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I pick up my green card at a USCIS office?
Generally no. Cards are mailed. Field offices can provide an I-551 stamp as temporary proof.

### What if my case status says delivered but I did not get it?
Contact USPS first, then USCIS. Keep records of every inquiry.

### Can I travel before the card arrives?
If you entered on an immigrant visa, the visa stamp serves as proof for one year. Otherwise, get an I-551 stamp before traveling.

### Is there a fee to resend a returned card?
If the card was returned to USCIS and is still available, re-mailing usually does not require a new application.

### How do I update my address?
Use the USCIS online change of address tool or Form AR-11, and update any pending cases.

### My card has a mistake. What now?
If the error was USCIS's, request a corrected card without a fee. If it came from your application, you may need to file Form I-90 with a fee.

### How long is the I-551 stamp valid?
The validity period is set by USCIS when the stamp is issued. Check the date written on it and request a new one if your card has still not arrived.
