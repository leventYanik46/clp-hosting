Wealthy investors seeking a U.S. green card now face a new question: EB-5 vs Gold Card. The EB-5 program has existed for decades and requires an at-risk investment that creates jobs. The Trump Gold Card, created by executive order in 2025, offers residence in exchange for a large contribution to the government. They differ in cost, legal footing, and risk.

## What the EB-5 program requires

EB-5 investors put capital into a U.S. business:

- **$800,000** in a targeted employment area (rural or high-unemployment) or infrastructure project
- **$1,050,000** elsewhere

The investment must create at least 10 full-time jobs for U.S. workers. Investors receive two-year conditional green cards, then file to remove conditions once the jobs are shown.

EB-5 has its own visa allocation. The 2022 EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act reserved a portion of visas for rural, high-unemployment, and infrastructure projects, which has helped some investors avoid backlogs. The regional center program is authorized through September 30, 2027.

## What the Gold Card involves

The Gold Card program was established by Executive Order 14351 in September 2025, and the administration launched an application process in December 2025. The framework includes:

- **A nonrefundable processing fee,** reported at $15,000 per applicant
- **A contribution of $1 million** for individuals, or $2 million when a company sponsors the applicant
- **Form I-140G,** an online-only petition with source-of-funds documentation
- **Classification within existing EB-1 or EB-2 categories,** rather than a new visa category

Because the Gold Card relies on EB-1 and EB-2 visa numbers, applicants from countries with long backlogs in those categories may still face waits.

## Key differences

| | EB-5 | Gold Card |
|---|---|---|
| Legal basis | Statute passed by Congress | Executive order |
| Money | Investment, potentially returned | Contribution, not returned |
| Job creation | Required | Not required |
| Visa category | EB-5 | EB-1 or EB-2 |
| Conditional residence | Yes, two years | Unclear |
| Track record | Decades of case law and regulations | New and evolving |

## The legal uncertainty

The biggest issue with the Gold Card is legal durability. Immigration categories and their requirements are set by Congress. Critics argue an executive order can't create a new path to permanent residence or substitute a payment for the statutory requirements of EB-1 or EB-2. Legal challenges and future policy changes are possible.

EB-5 also carries risks: the investment itself can lose money, and the regional center program needs reauthorization by 2027. But its legal framework is established, and investors with filed petitions have statutory protections.

## Who might consider each

**EB-5 makes sense if:**
- You want the possibility of getting your capital back
- You prefer a statutory, tested program
- Your nationality faces long EB-1 or EB-2 backlogs and a reserved EB-5 category is available

**The Gold Card may interest you if:**
- You prefer a contribution over managing an investment
- You don't want to rely on job creation
- You accept the legal uncertainty

## Questions to ask before committing

1. What is the current status of the Gold Card program and any litigation?
2. What does the visa bulletin show for EB-1, EB-2, and EB-5 for your country?
3. How will you document your source of funds under either program?
4. What happens to your family members under each program?

## Making a decision

For most investors, the established EB-5 program remains the more predictable choice today. The Gold Card is worth monitoring, but because it rests on an executive order and is new, we advise caution before committing funds. Rules and status have been changing, so confirm the current position before you rely on either path.

## Source of funds under both programs

Neither program accepts money without a documented lawful source. EB-5 adjudicators trace funds from origin to investment, often through several transfers and currency conversions. The Gold Card petition also requires evidence of the lawful source of the contribution. Investors who have prepared EB-5 source-of-funds documentation will find much of the work transferable, but the evidence will still need to be organized for the specific petition being filed.

## What family members should expect

EB-5 includes the spouse and unmarried children under 21 as derivatives on a single investment. Under the Gold Card framework, reports indicate that each family member requires a separate contribution, which multiplies the cost for larger families. For a family of four, that difference can change the comparison entirely. Confirm the current rules on dependents before making any decision.
