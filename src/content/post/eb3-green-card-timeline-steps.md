An EB-3 green card is an employer-sponsored green card for skilled workers, professionals with a bachelor's degree, and certain other workers. The honest answer to how long it takes is that it depends mostly on two things you do not control: Department of Labor processing and your country of birth. For most applicants the process runs from about two years to several years. For applicants born in India, the wait in line can stretch much longer.

## The EB-3 process, step by step

### 1. Prevailing wage determination

The employer asks the Department of Labor to set the minimum wage for the role. This stage alone has taken many months in recent years.

### 2. Recruitment

The employer runs the required recruitment to test the U.S. labor market: job order, newspaper ads, and additional steps for professional roles. There are mandatory waiting periods built in before the employer can file.

### 3. PERM labor certification

The employer files Form ETA-9089. DOL reviews it and either certifies it, audits it, or denies it. An audit adds significant time.

### 4. Form I-140

Once PERM is certified, the employer files the I-140 immigrant petition with USCIS within the certification's validity period. Premium processing is available and shortens this step to weeks.

### 5. Waiting for a current priority date

Your priority date is the date the PERM was filed. You can only move to the final stage when the State Department visa bulletin shows your date as current for EB-3 and your country of birth.

### 6. Adjustment of status or consular processing

If you are in the U.S., you file Form I-485. If you are abroad, the case moves to the National Visa Center and a consular interview. Either way, this final stage typically takes several more months.

## Why EB-3 for India takes so long

Each country can receive only about 7 percent of the green cards available in a category each year. Demand from India far exceeds that share in the employment categories, so Indian-born applicants form a long line. Applicants born in other countries may see their EB-3 dates move within months, while India's EB-3 dates may move slowly over years.

A few points that matter here:

- **Country of birth controls, not citizenship.** In some cases a spouse born in a different country can let both spouses use the spouse's country (cross-chargeability).
- **Porting between EB-2 and EB-3.** If you have an approved I-140 in one category and the other moves faster, you may be able to file a second I-140 and keep your original priority date.
- **Interim benefits.** H-1B holders with an approved I-140 can usually extend beyond the six-year limit while they wait.

## Steps to secure EB-3 sponsorship

The employer, not the worker, drives the case and must pay for the PERM process, including attorney fees and recruitment costs. To make sponsorship realistic:

- Confirm the role is permanent and full time
- Confirm you meet the job's minimum requirements as of the day you were hired, not just today
- Ask the employer about its sponsorship history and its plan for the timeline
- Keep your own copies of degrees, experience letters, and transcripts ready for the I-140

## What EB-3 sponsorship costs

On the employer side, costs include attorney fees, advertising, and the I-140 filing fee plus the Asylum Program Fee. Premium processing and the I-485 stage add more. DOL rules bar the employer from passing the PERM costs to the worker. Fees vary by firm and complexity, so ask for a written breakdown at the start.

## "Other workers" EB-3

A small slice of EB-3 is reserved for jobs that require less than two years of training or experience. These "other workers" visas are limited by law and often face longer waits than skilled worker cases.


## Frequently asked questions

### Can I change jobs during the EB-3 process?

Before the I-485 has been pending 180 days, changing employers usually means starting PERM over, although you may keep your priority date if the original I-140 was approved. After 180 days, AC21 portability may let you move to a same or similar job.

### Is EB-3 faster than EB-2?

Sometimes. The two categories move independently, and for some countries EB-3 has been current or ahead of EB-2 at times. Check both categories in the visa bulletin before deciding where to file.

### Can my spouse and children get green cards too?

Yes. A spouse and unmarried children under 21 can receive derivative green cards with the principal applicant, and they share the principal's priority date.
