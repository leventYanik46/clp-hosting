Family immigration covers petitions by U.S. citizens and permanent residents for spouses, children, parents, and siblings. Many cases are routine, but family histories often are not. Prior marriages, children born outside marriage, stepchildren, adoptions, past immigration violations, and aging-out deadlines all change what needs to be filed and when. A family immigration lawyer's work is to fit your family's real history into the categories the law allows.

## What a family immigration attorney handles

- **Spouse visas and marriage-based green cards**, inside the U.S. or through consular processing.
- **Fiancé(e) visas** and the choice between a K-1 and a spouse visa.
- **Parent petitions** by U.S. citizens 21 or older.
- **Child petitions**, including stepchildren and adopted children.
- **Sibling petitions**, which carry long waits.
- **Waivers** for relatives with past immigration or criminal issues.
- **Removing conditions** on a marriage-based green card.

## Where family cases get complicated

### Spouse visas

A spouse visa case turns on proof of a genuine marriage and on how the spouse entered the U.S. A spouse who entered with a visa can usually adjust status inside the country. A spouse who entered without inspection usually must process abroad and may need a provisional waiver. A spouse visa lawyer helps decide the route before anyone travels.

### Stepchildren and children born outside marriage

A stepchild qualifies only if the marriage creating the relationship happened before the child turned 18. A father petitioning for a child born outside marriage must show legitimation or a bona fide parent-child relationship. These rules trip up many families.

### Children approaching 21

Turning 21 can move a child into a different category with a much longer wait. The Child Status Protection Act can protect some children, but the formulas and deadlines are technical.

### Parents with immigration history

A U.S. citizen's parent who entered without inspection generally cannot adjust inside the U.S., and a citizen child is not a qualifying relative for the I-601A waiver. That leaves fewer options, and they need careful review.

### Sponsors with limited income

The petitioner must file an Affidavit of Support. If income falls short, a joint sponsor or household member may be needed.

## What working with a family immigration lawyer looks like

1. **Review of family history.** Every marriage, divorce, child, entry, and departure.
2. **Strategy.** Which petition, which order, and which route to the green card.
3. **Preparation.** Forms, evidence, translations, and the Affidavit of Support.
4. **Follow-through.** Requests for evidence, interviews, and the next steps after approval.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I sponsor my parents as a green card holder?
No. Only U.S. citizens 21 or older can sponsor parents.

### How long does a spouse visa take?
It depends on whether the petitioner is a citizen or permanent resident, where the spouse lives, and processing times.

### Can I sponsor a stepchild?
Yes, if your marriage to the child's parent happened before the child turned 18.

### What if my child turns 21 during the process?
The Child Status Protection Act may keep the child in their category. Check the calculation early.

### Do I need a lawyer to sponsor my spouse?
Not always. A lawyer is most useful when there are prior marriages, immigration violations, or questions about the route.

### Can a green card holder sponsor a married child?
No. Permanent residents can sponsor only spouses and unmarried children. A child who marries loses eligibility under a permanent resident's petition.

### Can I sponsor my brother or sister?
Yes, if you are a U.S. citizen 21 or older. Sibling petitions fall in the F4 category, which has long waits.

### What is a K-1 visa?
A fiancé(e) visa that lets a U.S. citizen's fiancé(e) enter to marry within 90 days and then apply for a green card.

### Can a family immigration lawyer help with a denied petition?
Yes. Denied family petitions can often be appealed to the Board of Immigration Appeals or refiled with stronger evidence.

### Can a family immigration lawyer handle the Affidavit of Support?
Yes. Preparing the I-864, choosing a joint sponsor when needed, and documenting income are part of most family cases.
