We also prepare <a href="/practice-area/key-services/l-1a-visa">L-1</a> transfers for Turkish companies opening U.S. offices, <a href="/practice-area/key-services/eb-2-niw-green-card">EB-2 National Interest Waiver</a> and <a href="/practice-area/key-services/eb-1a-green-card">EB-1A</a> green card petitions for academics, physicians, engineers and entrepreneurs, <a href="/practice-area/key-services/f-1-visa">F-1</a> student visas, and <a href="/practice-area/key-services/family-based-immigration">family-based immigrant visas</a> processed through the National Visa Center and the consulate.</p>
  <h2>How our Istanbul office works</h2>
  <p>Consultations in Istanbul are held in Turkish or English. Petitions are filed with USCIS from our U.S. offices, and we prepare clients for their consular interviews in Istanbul or Ankara. When a visa is refused under section 214(b) or held in administrative processing under section 221(g), we review the refusal, prepare the reapplication, and where appropriate file a <a href="/practice-area/key-services/immigration-mandamus">mandamus lawsuit</a> in U.S. federal court to compel a decision.</p>

  - question: Can Turkish citizens get an E-2 investor visa?
    answer: >-
      Yes. Türkiye has a qualifying treaty with the United States, so Turkish
      citizens who make a substantial, at-risk investment in a real U.S.
      business they will develop and direct can apply for the E-2 visa at the
      U.S. Consulate General in Istanbul.
  - question: Where will my visa interview take place?
    answer: >-
      Nonimmigrant visa interviews for residents of Türkiye are generally held at
      the U.S. Consulate General in Istanbul or the U.S. Embassy in Ankara.
      Immigrant visa interviews are scheduled by the National Visa Center at the
      post it designates. We prepare clients for either.
  - question: My visa was refused under 214(b). Can you help?
    answer: >-
      Yes. We review what happened at the interview, identify the weaknesses in
      the application and the ties you demonstrated, and prepare a stronger
      reapplication. For visas stuck in administrative processing under 221(g),
      we can file a mandamus lawsuit in U.S. federal court.
  - question: Do you handle green card cases for academics and professionals in Türkiye?
    answer: >-
      Yes. We prepare EB-2 National Interest Waiver and EB-1A self-petitions for
      Turkish academics, physicians, engineers and entrepreneurs, file them with
      USCIS from our U.S. offices, and manage consular processing in Istanbul or
      Ankara after approval.
