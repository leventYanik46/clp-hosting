Last updated: September 14, 2026

This extension is made by Capitol Law Partners for its own staff. It is not offered to the public and does nothing unless you are signed in to Counsel CRM, the firm's case management system, and to the EOIR Case Portal with your own account.

## What the extension does

When you press "Sync now" on the EOIR calendar card in Counsel CRM, the extension opens the EOIR Case Portal in a new tab. You sign in there yourself. Once you are signed in, the extension shows one button, "Sync to Counsel CRM". Pressing it reads your upcoming hearings list from the portal and sends it to Counsel CRM.

## What data it handles

- The upcoming hearings list from the EOIR Case Portal: hearing dates, times, courts, judges, hearing type, and the respondents' names and A-numbers. This is website content that includes personal information about the firm's clients.
- Which calendar you started syncing (an account number and EOIR ID), kept only for the current browser session.

## What it does not do

- It never sees, reads, or stores your EOIR password or your Counsel CRM password.
- It never reads any other page, document, recording, or file on the EOIR portal.
- It does not track your browsing, collect analytics, or show ads.
- It does not store anything on your computer beyond the current browser session.

## Where the data goes

The hearings list is sent only to Counsel CRM at counselcrm.com, which is operated by Capitol Law Partners on its own servers, using your own signed-in Counsel CRM session. It is not sent to the extension's developer or to any third party, and it is not sold.

## Who can use it

Only Capitol Law Partners staff with a firm Google Workspace account. The extension is distributed privately through the firm's Google Workspace and is not published for the public.
